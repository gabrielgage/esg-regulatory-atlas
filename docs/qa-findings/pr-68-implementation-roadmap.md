# PR 68 QA Finding - Regulation Implementation Roadmap

## Finding

Regulation detail pages already showed decision-readiness controls, source evidence and advisory prompts, but users still needed a clearer operational bridge from a potentially relevant record to a first planning sequence. Without that bridge, a user could understand the risk but still be unsure what to do in the next 30, 60 or 90 days.

## Resolution

- Added a reusable implementation-roadmap helper.
- Added a regulation-level 30/60/90-day roadmap to detail pages and drawers.
- Added suggested owner, source to verify, source posture and evidence focus cues.
- Added copyable roadmap Markdown with edition metadata and caveats.
- Added smoke and copy-surface coverage.

## Product Learning

The Atlas should not only answer “what is this regulation?” It should also answer “what should I verify and organize next?” Roadmaps make the product more useful for CSOs, legal teams, procurement leads, finance controllers and advisors without creating definitive compliance instructions.

## Legal And Data Caveat

Implementation roadmaps are orientation and planning aids. They do not determine applicability, verify legal completeness, provide legal advice or replace qualified legal, tax, investment or assurance review.
